FAQ

Common Questions

Do you work with clients outside Tanzania?
Yes. While we are based in Dar es Salaam and our primary focus is Tanzania and East Africa, we work with clients remotely across Africa and internationally. All communication and project management can be handled fully online.
Can you take over an existing codebase or project?
Yes, but we do a thorough code audit first. We need to understand what we're inheriting before committing to timelines or pricing. If the existing code is too problematic, we'll tell you honestly — even if that means recommending a rebuild.
How does payment work?
For fixed-price projects, we typically work on a 40% upfront / 40% at midpoint / 20% on delivery milestone structure. For retainers, monthly invoicing applies. We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and mobile banking. Payment terms are agreed upfront in writing.
Do you provide hosting and domain setup?
We can recommend, set up, and configure hosting for you. We'll always use reliable, appropriate hosting for your project's scale and budget. Domain registration is done in your name — you always retain full ownership of your assets.
What happens after the project is delivered?
Every project includes a minimum 3-month post-launch support period for bug fixes and small adjustments at no extra cost. After that, you can engage us on a retainer, hire us for enhancements, or move on fully — your choice. We hand over full code ownership and documentation regardless.
How quickly can you start?
Typically within 1–2 weeks of agreement and deposit. We run discovery in parallel with any final contract paperwork, so there's no dead time waiting for things to start moving. If you're urgent, tell us — we'll be upfront about whether we can accommodate your timeline.
