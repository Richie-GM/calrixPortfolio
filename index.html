ABOUT US

Who Is CalRix
Softworks?

CalRix Softworks is a Tanzanian software company founded in 2025 by Calvin and Richard — two Computer Science graduates from the Institute of Finance Management (IFM), Dar es Salaam. The name CalRix is a fusion of both founders, built on a shared belief: that Africa deserves world-class software built from within.

We design, develop, and deploy systems that solve real problems — starting with Tanzania, growing toward East Africa and beyond. We are bootstrapped, independent, and fully committed to the craft of building software that works.
